# Code of conduct

Treat contributors and users with respect. Welcome different backgrounds, communication styles, disabilities, and experience levels. Give specific, constructive feedback about work. Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, disclose private information, or collect someone else's voice data without consent.

Maintainers may remove harmful content and restrict participation to protect the community. Use GitHub's **Report content** action for abusive content or behavior, and its block controls when needed. Do not disclose private details in a public issue. Security and privacy vulnerabilities should go through [private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/madeordinary/within/security/advisories/new).
